# RNK: BCE + lambda * pairwise rank loss on report-mentioned pairs (folds 0/4 pilot, conditional base)

## Motivation (measured 2026-09-25, CPU only; scratchpad diag_loss.py)
- BCE fits target MAGNITUDES; the competition scores per-finding ORDERING (macro AUC).
- H-line evidence (2026-08-27_rank_loss.md, DINOv2, fold 0, batch 16): BCE + rank (rank_w 1.0) moved T1
  0.8700 -> 0.8722 and T1hq +0.0158; rank ALONE damaged noisy labels (Synovitis -0.0957): a pairwise loss
  learns every ordering in its target, and the BCE term protects against noise orderings. Never tested on R.
- Silent-cell down-weighting was considered and WITHDRAWN on data: on gold58 the silent Synovitis soft
  targets rank the host label (AUC 0.764, n=40) while the report-mentioned ones do not (0.491, n=18); R16's
  predictions on silent cells are not collapsed (sd 0.15-0.24). Fracture's silent cells hide host positives
  (7/30) with no ranking signal (0.497): a LABEL problem, not a loss problem.
- Expected informative pairs per batch of 8 among report-mentioned cells: Synovitis 0.2, Fracture 1.1,
  Contusion 1.8; 3.1-10.6 for the other nine findings.

## Treatment (the only change vs the base)
    L = BCE(all cells, dense soft targets, pos_weight)   [unchanged]   +   lambda * L_rank
    L_rank  the H-line `pairwise_rank_loss`, GLOBAL normalisation, no trim, copied verbatim (parity asserted):
            sum_{i,j} (y_i - y_j)_+ m_i m_j softplus(-(z_i - z_j)/tau) / sum_{i,j} (y_i - y_j)_+ m_i m_j,
            over the 8 studies of a batch and the 12 findings
    m       report-mentioned mask: (w_<finding> > 0) in artifacts/labels/labels_llm_public.parquet, binary.
            Pairs form ONLY between two mentioned cells; silent cells still train through BCE.
    lambda  0.5 (the H-line passed at 1.0 with batch 16; batch 8 sees roughly a quarter of the pairs)
    tau     1.0 (as the H-line)
    training only: evaluation windows, gold58 selection and deployment are unchanged; no new parameters.

## Decision rule, frozen (as REG)
Ruler: fixed-fused T1, deployment settings, paired study bootstrap, 4,000 draws per fold.
Pilot         FUND folds 1-3 iff mean(RNK - CTRL) >= +0.001 on folds 0,4 AND both folds positive.
Confirmation  (NOT queued) five-fold mean >= +0.001 AND folds 1-3 MEAN positive AND CI excludes 0.
A non-finite estimate, bootstrap draw or interval is INVALID (no verdict), never FAIL.
EXTRA CONDITION BEFORE ANY SUBMISSION (stated now): L_rank trains on report-derived orderings and T1 scores
report-derived labels, so a T1 gain here is MORE exposed to the ruler bias of the handover (s6.1) than REG.
A confirmed RNK ships only after ONE leaderboard comparison against the incumbent; no offline ruler clears it.
A FAIL closes THIS recipe only (lambda 0.5, mentioned pairs, global normalisation, tau 1.0).
